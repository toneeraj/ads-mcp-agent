# AI-First Design — contents

**Purpose:** the authoritative index of the book — its standing domains, standing principles, chapters and house rules. Every chapter is placed here before it is written.

## Standing domains

Every concept is stated without reference to any system, then read against all three.

| Domain | The decision being made |
|---|---|
| Marketplace pricing | What price a listing carries against competitors, between a floor and a ceiling |
| Advertising bids | What a click is worth, and what budget a campaign may spend |
| Claims adjudication | Whether a claim is paid, against the policy in force on the day of loss |

## Standing principles

| Principle | Held in |
|---|---|
| A deterministic engine decides; a reasoning layer proposes | Ch 1 |
| The design authority and the implementer are separate hands | Ch 6 |
| No abstraction before a second instance | Ch 5 |

## House rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter shape | Scene, concept, one block, two scenes, what it paid, the gap, to graduate |
| Titles | State the claim, never the subject |
| Scenes | One concrete moment; sharpen the scene before adding rows |
| Refused | Source-shaped prose, a matrix per domain, meta sections, open questions, definitions, prose restating a block, coverage for its own sake |
| Gaps | Point at the chapter that will hold them |
| Diagrams | Text blocks are authoritative; Mermaid renders from them, inline |
| Employer material | No employer system names, figures or code; experiments appear only as generic scenes |
