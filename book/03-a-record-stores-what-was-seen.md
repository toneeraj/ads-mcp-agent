# 3 · A record stores what was seen, not what was concluded

**A decision record keeps the observation and its age. A conclusion stored in its place cannot be re-checked when the observation goes stale.**

**Scene to write:** Marketplace pricing — a floor computed from a competitor offer on a listing that had gone inactive days earlier.
